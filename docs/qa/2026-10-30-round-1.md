### Pitch candidates

Each is under 20 words, uses no idiom, promises nothing unbuilt, and claims no coverage beyond "a watched app". Read as each persona:

1. "tos.watch monitors terms of service and privacy policies and emails you the exact words that change." (16 words)
   - Skimmer: gets it. Skeptic: "monitors" reads as all of them, so it implies coverage we don't have. Mobile: short enough. Literal reader: "the words that change" is clear. Breaker: nothing to break. Weakness: no limit on which apps.
2. "Terms of service and privacy policy monitor: we email you the exact words when a watched app changes them." (19 words)
   - Skimmer: names the thing first. Skeptic: "a watched app" limits the claim to what is watched, and "exact words" is what the check verifies. Mobile: wraps to three lines at 375px, readable in a link preview. Literal reader: "them" refers to the two documents named first, which is clear enough; "watched" is explained by the site name line. Breaker: nothing.
3. "Free email alerts when a watched app changes its terms of service or privacy policy, with the exact words." (19 words)
   - Skimmer: clear, but "free email alerts" leads with price, not what it is. Skeptic: fine. Mobile: fine. Literal reader: "with the exact words" dangles. Breaker: "free" is a promise that must stay true.

**Chosen: 2.** It names the product category first (the operator's request), limits the claim to watched apps, and has no dangling phrase. It is the description in the home page link preview.
